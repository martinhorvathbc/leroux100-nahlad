The performance, moment by moment
  1. 0:00:00 In the dark, a first voice. The light slowly awakens.
  2. 0:01:21 The narration spreads out over the auditorium. The light rises slowly, without big effects.
  3. 0:08:11 Erik’s voice is heard for the first time from the sound of the hall, and the light turns red.
  4. 0:10:19 Drama: dramatic light and a sound that carries the conflict.
  5. 0:15:40 The actors play live, the sound places them in space. The sound carries the narration over the whole audience.
  6. 0:17:00 On the roof of the Opera: a conversation in the half-light.
  7. 0:22:07 A musical interlude: the music alone, in the middle of the performance.
  8. 0:25:59 The Phantom appears: the stage turns red.
  9. 0:32:15 Sound and light darken, the Phantom’s red fills the hall.
  10. 0:34:02 The tension rises.
  11. 0:40:48 The tension holds, the red no longer lets go of the hall.
  12. 0:49:48 The Phantom’s red and Raoul’s blue: two worlds facing each other.
  13. 0:58:15 Silence. Then the sound falls from above, from all sides, and converges in the centre of the hall.
  14. 1:00:03 Underground: red alternates with blue in sharp tension.
  15. 1:06:08 The hall goes dark. The last act stays in red, more muted.
  16. 1:11:53 The light gradually brightens to white: forgiveness, reconciliation.
  17. 1:14:02 Epilogue: white and red answer each other, and the last voice fills the whole hall.

2 · At your venue

This is how it would look at your venue

Castle, black box, church: the same arrangement can be set up in any space. Your hall does not have to be a traditional theatre.

Illustrations, for guidance only: the layout adapts to your space.

Your hall

You will not see the Phantom. He will surround you with his voice.

The stage stands in the centre of the hall, with the audience seated around it. The Phantom’s voice comes from loudspeakers around the edge: at times it travels across the hall over people’s heads, at others it sounds from every side at once. Subwoofers directly beneath the stage carry the bass.

This is how the version was first performed, in spatial sound: at the premiere on 18 October 2026 in the Golden Hall, Šimák Zámok Pezinok.

NUIT LEROUX

Illustration

On 15 April 2027, leave box no. 5 empty.

In Gaston Leroux’s novel, this box belongs to the Phantom and to no one else. Exactly a hundred years after the author’s death, every theatre that joins us leaves it free for him: one evening, one hour, and the Phantom’s voice heard in theatres all over Europe.

It is an invitation, not yet a programme. We are shaping it together with the theatres that join us. Our ambition: a hundred theatres, a hundred points of light on the map.

No boxes in your hall? Leave seat no. 5 free.
