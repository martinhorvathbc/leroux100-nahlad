Six folders

  1. 01

    Master audio

    • Original music in separate tracks (stems).
    • Reference vocals in MP3.
    • The Phantom’s original tracks, mixed in 5.1 surround sound.
    • Reference voice recordings of the Phantom.
    Size: to be confirmed
  2. 02

    Post-production standard

    • Processed and mixed in the mixing hall of KC Arténa STVR in Bratislava.
    • A hall certified for Dolby Atmos Features, with an Avid S6 console: the only certified Dolby Atmos mixing hall in Slovakia (according to KC Arténa).
    Size: to be confirmed
  3. 03

    Timeline and show control

    • A complete timeline that breaks down the sound and lighting cues.
    • The complete technical script.
    • A separate voiceover for the recorded Phantom.
    • Further supporting elements.
    Size: to be confirmed
  4. 04

    Scripts and materials for the cast

    • The complete script of the performance.
    • The script of the opening choral scene, before the performance begins.
    • A reading script for the actors, for rehearsals.
    • Author’s texts and bridges.
    Size: to be confirmed
  5. 05

    Technical rider and layout

    • Implementation drawing.
    • Stage position.
    • Loudspeaker layout.
    • Sound rider to the Slovak production standard.
    Size: to be confirmed
  6. 06

    Communication materials

    • Complete PR materials.
    • Complete promotional materials.
    Size: to be confirmed
