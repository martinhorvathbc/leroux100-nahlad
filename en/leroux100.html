LEROUX100

A Slovak adaptation of Gaston Leroux’s novel as an immersive stage interpretation, now offered as an international version for the centenary.

The idea

In the dark, a voice

The Phantom never appears. We only hear him. Music and spatial sound carry the evening; the Phantom’s voice moves around the hall, and his box, no. 5, stays empty.

LEROUX100 is the new international version of this adaptation: a text for five voices after the novel, with original music and sound design created for the dark. Concept and voice of the Phantom: Martin Horváth.
